दृष्टि Drishti — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Ikshana Solutions LLC

Drishti is a Vedic astrology app. It calculates your birth chart (kundli) and lets you ask an AI astrologer questions about it. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell your data.

What you enter

Name, optional gender, date, time and place of birth for each profile you add (yourself, family or friends). Only add other people's details with their permission.

Where it is stored

Your profiles and chat history are stored on your device (browser/app storage). There is no account and no login. Deleting a profile, clearing chat history in the app, or uninstalling the app removes them.

What is sent to our server, and why

  • Chart calculation: birth date, time, coordinates and time zone are sent to our server to compute the chart, matching and dashas. The server does not keep them after replying.
  • City search: the letters you type are sent to look up cities in our bundled city list (GeoNames). No GPS is used.
  • Wallet: a random device ID (not linked to your name, phone number or email) is stored on our server with your credit balance, free-question count and a log of credit changes.

AI answers

When you ask a question, the question, your recent messages in that chat, the profile name and the computed chart (birth date, time, place and planetary positions) are sent to our AI provider to generate the answer. We only use AI providers whose API terms say customer data is not used to train their models. We do not store your questions or answers on our server.

AI answers are astrological guidance for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. For those, please talk to a qualified professional.

Payments

The current version has no real payments (credits are demo only). When paid credits launch, payments will be handled by the App Store, Google Play or a payment gateway such as Razorpay. We never see or store your card details.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. Drishti has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking.

Children

Drishti is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Parents may add a child's birth details as a family profile; that data stays on the parent's device except as described above.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any profile at any time in the app. To delete your wallet record, email us your device ID (shown under Profile) and we will remove it.
