दृष्टि Drishti — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Ikshana Solutions LLC, 273 Lewis Fisher Dr, Hopewell NJ 08560

This policy describes the Drishti app and website operated by Ikshana Solutions LLC. We collect as little as we can, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. See also the Terms of Service and Support.

Who this covers

People who use the chart, matching, horoscope, and Acharya question features, and astrologers who sign in to offer live chat or voice. On this iOS site, Acharya questions are Apple In-App Purchases. Live-session wallet top-ups are Stripe. Astrologer payouts are PayPal or Stripe. The Android app uses Google Play for Acharya questions; that wording is shown only inside the Android app.

Information we collect

  • Profile: name, optional gender, birth date, birth time, and birth place (city, coordinates, and time zone) for you or for a family member you add. Add someone else’s details only with their permission.
  • Account: if you sign in as an astrologer, name, email, a password stored only as a hash, languages, specialties, a short bio, an optional photo, and your per-minute rate.
  • Chats: Acharya questions and the recent messages needed to answer them. Live-session messages, the question, and the chart you choose to share, tied to a random device id.
  • Calls: voice is a live room on LiveKit. We send a short join token. We do not record the call. LiveKit carries the audio between the two people only while the session is open.
  • Purchases: for an Acharya question, a random device id and the store’s transaction or order id, so one purchase answers one question. We do not receive your card number.
  • Wallet: Stripe PaymentIntent id, amount, and device id for a top-up. Balance is stored in cents. We never receive the card number.
  • Payouts: a Stripe account id or a PayPal email for an astrologer. Bank details and identity documents go to Stripe or PayPal, not to our database.

Why we use it

  • To calculate a chart, match, dasha, panchang, or horoscope from the birth details you enter.
  • To answer an Acharya question with an AI model, using the question, recent chat, profile name, and computed chart.
  • To run a live chat or voice session, bill it per minute from the wallet, and pay the astrologer.
  • To prevent one purchase from being reused, to refund an unused wallet balance, and to keep payment and tax records.
  • To look up a city name in our bundled city list. We do not use GPS.

Acharya questions

  • Acharya questions: a random device ID is stored with the Google Play order id for each paid question, so one purchase can answer only one question. The purchase token is kept only until Google Play accepts that the question was delivered, then it is deleted. We keep the order id and a one-way hash of the token. We never receive your card number, UPI id, or Google password.
  • Acharya questions: a random device ID is stored with the App Store transaction id for each paid question, so one purchase can answer only one question. The signed transaction is kept only until Apple accepts that the question was delivered, then it is deleted. We keep the transaction id and a one-way hash. We never receive your card number or Apple ID password.
  • आचार्य प्रश्न: हर सशुल्क प्रश्न के साथ एक यादृच्छिक डिवाइस आईडी और App Store लेनदेन आईडी रखी जाती है, ताकि एक खरीदारी केवल एक प्रश्न का उत्तर दे। हस्ताक्षरित लेनदेन तभी तक रहता है जब तक Apple स्वीकार न कर ले कि प्रश्न दिया जा चुका है, फिर वह हट जाता है। हम लेनदेन आईडी और उसका एक-तरफ़ा हैश रखते हैं। हम आपका कार्ड नंबर या Apple ID पासवर्ड नहीं लेते।

Payments

Each question to Acharya costs $0.99, paid as a Google Play consumable (one purchase, one question). There is no free question and no credit pack. Google Play handles the payment. We never see or store your card details.

आचार्य का हर प्रश्न $0.99 का है, Google Play से (एक खरीदारी, एक प्रश्न)। कोई मुफ़्त प्रश्न नहीं और कोई क्रेडिट पैक नहीं। भुगतान Google Play करता है। हम आपका कार्ड विवरण नहीं देखते और नहीं रखते।

Each question to Acharya costs $0.99, paid as an Apple In-App Purchase through the App Store (one purchase, one question). There is no free question and no credit pack. Apple handles the payment. We never see or store your card details.

आचार्य का हर प्रश्न $0.99 का है, App Store से (एक खरीदारी, एक प्रश्न)। कोई मुफ़्त प्रश्न नहीं और कोई क्रेडिट पैक नहीं। भुगतान Apple करता है। हम आपका कार्ड विवरण नहीं देखते और नहीं रखते।

Live astrologer sessions are paid from a wallet you top up with Stripe. A session places a five-minute hold, then bills per minute. The unused hold returns to the wallet. If nobody accepts within two minutes, the hold is returned. Astrologers are paid by PayPal or Stripe. The listing plan, when offered on the website, is a Stripe subscription.

The birth chart, life periods, dosha, panchang, matching, daily horoscope and kundli PDF are free and do not require a purchase.

Who else receives information

  • Apple processes Acharya In-App Purchases on iPhone. We send Apple only what the App Store purchase flow requires.
  • Google Play processes Acharya purchases in the Android app.
  • Stripe processes wallet top-ups, listing subscriptions on the website, and bank payouts. Stripe receives the card or bank details. We do not.
  • PayPal receives the PayPal email when an astrologer chooses PayPal for payouts.
  • LiveKit carries live call audio for the length of the session. We do not record it.
  • The AI provider receives the Acharya question, recent messages, profile name, and chart so it can write the answer. We use providers whose API terms say customer content is not used to train their models.
  • Vercel and our database host the app and store the records described above.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

AI answers

Acharya questions and answers are not stored on our server after the reply is sent. Live-session messages are stored, because both people need the thread.

AI answers and live sessions are astrological guidance for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

How long we keep it

A one-off chart request is not kept after the reply. Acharya purchase records (transaction or order id, and a hash) are kept so a purchase cannot be reused. Wallet, payout, and tax records are kept as required for refunds, disputes, and taxes. Live-session messages and the shared chart stay until the account is deleted. Profiles and Acharya chats on the phone stay until you delete them there.

Deletion

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. That removes profiles and Acharya chats on the phone, live-session messages, and the shared chart. The remaining Stripe wallet balance is refunded to the original card. We keep payment, payout, and tax records with the device id removed from the wallet ledger. Card numbers are never stored. An astrologer deletes the provider account from Astrologer mode, Settings. That removes the public listing. Payout and tax records are kept.

Children

Drishti is not directed at children. You must be 18 or older to buy an Acharya question, add wallet money, or join a live session. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. Drishti has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking.
