Ask Acharya, $0.99

On iPhone, each Acharya question is a $0.99 Apple In-App Purchase. One purchase answers one question. The chart, periods, matching, horoscope, and kundli PDF stay free. You are charged only when an answer starts.

On Android, each Acharya question is a $0.99 Google Play purchase. One purchase answers one question. The chart, periods, matching, horoscope, and kundli PDF stay free. You are charged only when an answer starts. The live-session wallet cannot pay for Acharya.

If a question is not answered

If the answer never starts, that purchase is kept for a retry. Ask again with the same purchase. You are not charged a second time for that question.

Live astrologer billing

Chat and voice use a wallet you top up with Stripe. A session places a five-minute hold, then bills per minute at the astrologer’s rate, the same rate for chat and call. End anytime. The unused hold returns to the wallet. If nobody accepts within two minutes, the hold is returned in full.

The wallet is in US dollars and pays only for live chat or voice sessions with a person. In the Android app, keep Drishti open and the screen on during a call, and a finished chat cannot be opened again.

Delete your account

Open Profile and tap Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm. This removes profiles and chats on this phone and the live sessions stored for this device. Any remaining Stripe wallet balance is refunded to the original card, usually in 5–10 business days. If a session is still open, end it first.

How astrologers are paid

Astrologers keep 95% of each live session. Drishti keeps 5%. Payouts are sent by PayPal or Stripe on the weekly payout. Amounts under $1.00 are not sent. The listing plan, when it is offered, is purchased on the website, not inside the iPhone app.Astrologers register and buy the listing plan on the website, not inside the Android app. The app offers sign-in only.
